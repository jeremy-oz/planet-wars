# Mood board

Eight references for the direction in `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md` ("quiet space, living light"). Rendered
locally with FLUX.2-klein 4B (Apache-2.0) from the prompts below, so they are ours to keep in the repo.
They stand in for Auralux / Eufloria screenshots, which we do not redistribute.

| # | File | What to take from it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-calm-space.png` | The void. Almost nothing; a faint teal haze is the most colour a background gets. |
| 2 | `02-glowing-planets.png` | The four player colours plus neutral as soft discs with halos. |
| 3 | `03-growth-ring.png` | One planet, one thin ring three-quarters full: the growth ring. |
| 4 | `04-fleet-streams.png` | Fleets as rivers of tiny lights between planets, trails fading behind. |
| 5 | `05-capture-ripple.png` | A capture: a flash and concentric ripples expanding into the dark. |
| 6 | `06-organic-soft.png` | Eufloria's softness: things drift, breathe and blur at the edges. |
| 7 | `07-nebula-parallax.png` | A background you could play over: low-contrast layered clouds. |
| 8 | `08-ui-glass.png` | The HUD: dark glass panels with thin glowing rims and lots of empty space. |

Regenerate: `tools/` has no image dependencies on purpose; the prompts live in this file's history and
the style guide, and any local or hosted image model will do. Keep the output at 1024×640.
